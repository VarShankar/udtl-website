What we do

From numerics to digital twins

The Utah Digital Twin Lab (UDTL) develops provably-accurate, scalable scientific machine learning architectures that help create digital twins for scientific discovery and engineering. We work where scientific machine learning, scientific computing, high performance computing, and applied mathematics meet.

A useful digital twin has to be fast enough to query thousands of times, faithful to the physics it represents, and honest about its own error. We pursue all three, building on two decades of work on kernel and meshless methods.
