What we look for

Students who do well here like both the mathematics and the code. Typical backgrounds include numerical analysis and PDEs, approximation theory, machine learning, and scientific or high performance computing. You don't need all of them, but you should want to learn the rest.

How to reach out

  • Prospective PhD students: apply to the Kahlert School of Computing PhD program and name Prof. Shankar in your application. The Scientific Computing track is a natural fit.
  • Current Utah students (BS/MS): email a CV, transcript, and a short note about which of our research thrusts interests you, and why.
  • Postdocs and visitors: email with a CV and a brief research statement.
