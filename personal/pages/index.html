Scientific machine learning · Numerical methods · HPC

Varun Shankar

Assistant Professor, Kahlert School of Computing, University of Utah. Director of the Utah Digital Twin Lab.

I build efficient, scalable, and provably-accurate machine learning models for scientific applications. My work sits where scientific machine learning meets scientific computing and high performance computing: neural and kernel operators for surrogate modeling, learning architectures that preserve physical structure by construction, and high-order kernel and meshless methods for PDEs on complicated and moving geometries.

I joined the faculty on the tenure track in July 2023. Before that I was an Assistant Professor (Lecturer) in the Kahlert School of Computing (2018–2023), where I helped build and later co-direct the Master of Software Development program. I received my PhD in Computing (Scientific Computing) from the University of Utah and did a four-year postdoc in the Math Biology group in Utah's Department of Mathematics.
