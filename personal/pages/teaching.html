Course design

CS 6018, Application System Design. I designed the capstone course of the MSD program. It uses Android development to teach software design and engineering, and moved from Java to Kotlin in 2022. I co-maintain it, and CS 4530, with Prof. Ben Jones.

CS 6013, Systems I. In 2020–21 I added GPU and multicore programming to the course.

Numerical analysis. For Math 5620/6865 I wrote a unified treatment of the numerical analysis of ODEs and PDEs, along with my own typeset notes. I later adapted it, with numerical linear algebra and data-science methods added, for CS 3200 and CS 6220 / COMP 5960.
